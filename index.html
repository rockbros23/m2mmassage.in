Before you book

Good questions.

Still unsure about something? Message us on WhatsApp — a real person replies, usually within 30 minutes.

Talk to us
Is this a therapeutic massage?

Yes. Every session is professional therapeutic massage delivered by a certified male therapist. M2M is a wellness studio, not an escort service. Any inappropriate request ends the session immediately, with full payment retained.

Will my therapist be male?

Always. M2M is a male-to-male massage studio, and all our therapists are men. If you have a preferred therapist, request them when booking — subject to availability.

Do you offer outcall massage?

We do — to homes and hotels across Mumbai for a ₹500 surcharge. You'll need a private space, a flat surface, and a government ID for the therapist's safety. Outcall availability is confirmed on WhatsApp.

What do I wear? Will I be draped?

Undress to whatever feels comfortable — many clients keep their underwear on. You'll be professionally draped at all times, with only the area being worked exposed. Glutes are worked only over the sheet, and only with your consent.

How discreet is it, really?

The studio is unmarked and private, with a separate entrance. We don't photograph clients, we don't share names or numbers, and appointment details are deleted after 90 days. Outcall therapists arrive in plain clothes with an unbranded kit.

What is your cancellation policy?

Reschedule or cancel free of charge up to 12 hours before your session. Inside 12 hours, 50% is payable. No-shows are charged in full. If we ever need to cancel, your session is free.
