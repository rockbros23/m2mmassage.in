Our story

Built for men.
Run with care.

M2M began in 2019 in a one-room studio in Bandra, with two therapists and a simple observation: men were showing up tense, apologetic, and unsure whether they were allowed to ask for what they needed.

Why M2M exists

A room where
nobody performs.

Most massage spaces weren't designed with men in mind. The music is wrong, the pressure is polite, and the small talk never stops.

We wanted somewhere quieter. A studio where you could arrive with a stiff back and a bad week, say two sentences, and be understood. Where the therapist is male, the pressure is honest, and nothing about the hour needs to be explained.

Seven years and more than 1,200 sessions later, the room is bigger but the idea hasn't changed. We still ask before we touch. We still adjust pressure when you ask. We still don't keep photos, share names, or make anyone feel like they're in the wrong place.

Men come to us for sports recovery, desk damage, grief, insomnia, and simple exhaustion. Some come for their first massage ever. All of them get the same thing: an hour that is genuinely theirs.

All men are welcome here. Every body type and experience level. Respect and consent run in both directions.

What we believe

Four things we
won't compromise.

Discretion

No signage, no photos, no shared client lists. Booking details are deleted after 90 days and your therapist never discusses clients outside the studio.

Consent

Pressure, position, areas worked — you're asked, not assumed. Either of you can pause or stop at any time, without explanation or awkwardness.

Craft

Certified therapists, monthly training, anonymous reviews after every session. Good hands are made by practice, not personality.

Welcome

First massage or five-hundredth. Athletic, soft, hairy, shy, tattooed, older, uncertain — the room is built to hold all of it.

Standards

Hygiene isn't
a footnote.

01

Fresh linen, every body

Sheets, towels, robes and face-cradle covers are changed between every client and laundered at 60°C or above.

02

Surfaces and tools

Tables, stones, bowls and door handles are sanitised after each session with hospital-grade products. Oils are decanted, never double-dipped.

03

Air and water

HEPA-filtered air in every room, drinking water and showers filtered and cleaned daily. No scented candles if you'd rather not.

04

Therapist health

Therapists don't work with colds, cuts or skin conditions. Hands and forearms are washed before and after every session, no exceptions.
